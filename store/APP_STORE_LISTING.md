## Description (max 4000 chars)
```
Book Scanner turns your phone into a fast, organized book-digitizing tool. Photograph any page and Book Scanner recognizes the text on-device, compresses the image, and keeps everything neatly filed in the cloud so it’s on all your devices.

BUILT FOR REAL READING AND STUDY
Organize your scans the way a library works: Projects hold Books, and Books hold Pages. Keep a research project, a course, or a whole shelf in order without a mess of camera-roll photos.

ON-DEVICE TEXT RECOGNITION
Every page you scan is run through on-device OCR, so the recognized text stays with the page — ready to read, edit, and reuse. Chinese and Latin scripts are both supported. Recognition runs on your device; no page is sent to a third-party OCR service.

EDIT THE TEXT
OCR is a starting point, not the final word. Open any page to review and correct the recognized text in a full editor, then save. Re-run recognition any time.

EXPORT TO PDF
Turn any book into a clean, multi-page PDF with one tap — perfect for sharing, printing, or archiving.

ORGANIZE FREELY
- Drag to reorder pages within a book
- Select and move pages between books
- Rename or delete projects, books, and pages

SMALL FILES, FAST SYNC
Photos are automatically compressed before upload, so a full page is typically under a few hundred kilobytes — quick to sync and easy on storage — while staying sharp enough to read.

SIGN IN, SYNC EVERYWHERE
Create an account with your email and your projects, books, pages, and recognized text sync securely to the cloud. Sign in on another device and everything is there.

PRIVACY
Your scans are stored in your own private, per-account cloud space. Only you can access your data. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Book Scanner is perfect for students, researchers, teachers, and anyone who wants to capture and keep the text from the books they read.
```
